# THE THICK OF ITCH

**Episode:** 203
**Title:** The National Delivery Doormat
**Date:** 2026-09-11
**Genre:** Original political satire

---

## COLD OPEN

The Minister arrives at the Cabinet room and stops with one foot in the air.

There is a new doormat outside the door.

It says **WELCOME TO DELIVERY**.

Underneath, in smaller letters, it says **SUBJECT TO CLEARANCE**.

"Why is there a constitutional carpet?"

The Permanent Secretary looks down at it as if admiring a bridge.

"It is the National Delivery Doormat."

"That is a sentence with no load-bearing words."

The comms director crouches to photograph it from floor level.

"It creates a threshold moment."

"It creates a trip hazard with branding."

The policy lead points to the fibres.

"Each department must wipe its commitments here before entering the room."

"Wipe them?"

"To remove dust, ambiguity, and uncontrolled expectation."

The junior official arrives carrying a small sign.

"Facilities would like to know whether mud counts as stakeholder evidence."

The Permanent Secretary nods.

"Only if it is dry."

## ACT ONE

The doormat began as a facilities request after several departments tracked uncertainty into the Cabinet room.

By nine o'clock it had become a cross-government entry protocol.

By half past nine it had a laminated usage guide.

By ten it had been described as "a place-based delivery interface" by someone who had once chaired a workshop on doors.

The Minister reads the briefing.

"The mat allows commitments to transition from external aspiration to internal readiness."

"It is where shoes go," says the junior official.

"Shoes carry pressure," says the policy lead.

"So do voters. We do not ask them to scrape themselves on the way in."

Legal is worried that standing on **DELIVERY** may be interpreted as a binding undertaking.

Finance is worried that wiping feet suggests there is something to clean up.

Comms is worried the word **WELCOME** may imply the government has opened something.

"Could we change it to **APPROACHING DELIVERY**?" asks the comms director.

"No," says the Minister. "We are not putting the country on a doorstep and calling it momentum."

The Permanent Secretary suggests a pilot entry.

The Minister steps onto the mat.

It says nothing.

Everyone waits.

The junior official checks the packaging.

"It is reversible."

They flip it over.

The other side says **PLEASE COME BACK AFTER THE REVIEW**.

## ACT TWO

Departments begin requesting special access arrangements.

Transport wants a longer mat for phased arrival.

Health wants one that can absorb winter pressure.

Education asks whether pupils can be taught the difference between wiping, pausing, and reform.

Treasury sends a note saying it supports the doormat provided nobody treats crossing it as commitment, acceleration, approval, entitlement, or a spend event.

The Permanent Secretary convenes the Threshold Assurance Group.

The Minister looks at the agenda.

"Item one: determine whether entering the room constitutes progress."

"It may constitute progress towards a discussion," says the policy lead.

"So does walking into a cupboard."

The comms director proposes a slogan: **Step Into Delivery**.

Legal says that may imply delivery is physically present.

Finance says physical presence may have estate implications.

The junior official notices a small brush attached to the wall.

"What is this for?"

"Legacy commitments," says the Permanent Secretary.

"We brush them off?"

"We refresh their presentation."

The Minister looks at the brush.

"That is sweeping with a press release."

Comms pauses, then decides not to write it down.

## ACT THREE

By lunchtime the doormat has become a queue.

No department wants to be seen arriving late to delivery, so officials line the corridor holding folders at shoe height.

The policy lead unveils a threshold dashboard.

Green means a commitment has crossed the mat.

Amber means a commitment is on the mat but facing the wrong way.

Red means a commitment has wiped once and requires narrative support.

Grey means the commitment is still in the porch.

The Minister studies the chart.

"There is no column for whether anyone did the thing."

"That is beyond the current threshold scope," says the Permanent Secretary.

A junior press officer appears with panic in both hands.

"The cameras are asking why the doormat says delivery when the announcement says preparation."

The comms director closes her eyes.

"Can we say preparation is the welcoming phase of delivery?"

"Only if we have decided words no longer have owners," says the Minister.

Legal asks whether the public may slip on mixed messaging.

Finance asks whether slips are insurable.

The junior official lifts one corner of the mat.

Underneath is an old memo.

It says:

**STOP ANNOUNCING ENTRANCES. START OPENING DOORS.**

No one speaks for a moment.

The Minister pockets it.

## CLIMAX

In the press room, the doormat has been placed in front of the lectern, where it looks like it is waiting to be blamed.

A reporter asks whether the government now believes walking over a slogan counts as delivery.

The comms director smiles with the controlled bravery of someone presenting a wet umbrella as infrastructure.

The Permanent Secretary holds the Threshold Assurance lines in both hands.

The Minister does not take them.

"A doorway is not a policy. A mat is not a programme. A photo of arrival is not arrival. Delivery starts when people know what will change, when it will change, who is responsible, and what we are no longer pretending can happen at the same time."

The room goes quiet.

The junior official brings him the memo from under the mat.

The Minister reads it aloud.

"Stop announcing entrances. Start opening doors."

Then he opens the press room door and points down the corridor.

"Right. Who owns the first one?"

For the first time all morning, nobody looks at the mat.

## TAG

Back in the Cabinet room, Facilities removes the doormat.

Underneath is a cleaner rectangle of carpet.

The junior official looks at it.

"At least something changed."

The Minister nods.

"Put that in the minutes."

**END**
