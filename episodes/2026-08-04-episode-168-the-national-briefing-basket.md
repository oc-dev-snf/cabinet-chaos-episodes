# THE THICK OF ITCH

**Episode:** 168
**Title:** The National Briefing Basket
**Genre:** Original political satire

---

## COLD OPEN

At 07:00, the Prime Minister announced the National Briefing Basket.

It was described as a practical container for all important information that was not yet ready to become knowledge.

Communications said the public wanted joined-up government.

Policy said joined-up government required somewhere to put the loose ends.

Treasury asked whether the basket was new spending or repurposed optimism.

Facilities said there was already a basket in the Cabinet Office, but it was being used for lanyards, extension leads, and one historical commitment nobody could lift.

Legal asked whether placing a briefing in a basket made it discoverable, retrievable, or merely unattended.

Digital said the basket could have a dashboard.

The Cabinet Secretary said the basket would demonstrate grip by keeping all ungripped material in one visible place.

The first approved basket categories were:

URGENT.

NEARLY URGENT.

URGENT AFTER CLEARANCE.

NOT URGENT BUT ANXIOUS.

The junior adviser asked whether a basket full of unresolved briefings was still a briefing system.

The room agreed it was at least more system than the floor.

## ACT ONE

By 08:30, departments had started contributing to the Briefing Basket.

Transport submitted a route map, a timetable, and a note explaining why neither should be seen together.

Health submitted a briefing with a reassuring cover sheet and several less reassuring appendices.

Education submitted three bullet points, all beginning with "standards", and requested that the fourth bullet be inferred from tone.

The Home Office submitted a sealed envelope marked FOR CONTEXT ONLY, then asked for it to be kept at the top without being opened.

Treasury submitted a costing note which concluded the cheapest basket was a smaller expectation.

Communications wanted the basket on camera.

Policy warned that a visible basket implied contents.

Communications said contents were helpful if blurred.

Legal said blur could be interpreted as concealment.

Digital proposed a transparency filter.

The Cabinet Secretary asked whether transparency filters made things more transparent.

Digital said they made transparency more configurable.

The junior adviser suggested reading the most important briefing before announcing the basket.

There was a short silence while everyone waited to see whether important had been cleared by the relevant owners.

## ACT TWO

At 10:00, the basket entered operational rehearsal.

Every briefing now required a Basket Suitability Note, a Handle Confidence Rating, and a Contents Sensitivity Tag.

The Prime Minister practiced the morning line.

"We are bringing order to the business of government," the Prime Minister said.

Communications placed a briefing in the basket.

It read: ORDER IS BEING DELIVERED THROUGH A NEW PROCESS FOR ORDER.

Policy said it had the shape of an answer without the risk of one.

Treasury approved the shape.

Legal asked whether order had consented to be delivered.

The Cabinet Secretary said the key benefit was prioritisation.

Digital displayed a screen showing each briefing as received, reviewed, reclassified, carried forward, or awaiting basket confidence.

The Prime Minister tried again.

"People deserve clarity."

The basket label changed to: CLARITY IN PROGRESS.

Communications asked for something warmer.

Policy changed it to: CLARITY WITH A HUMAN FACE.

Legal asked whose face.

The Cabinet Secretary said faces were outside scope unless already funded.

## ACT THREE

At noon, the basket was full.

Transport had added a second map to explain why the first map was not misleading if viewed from a policy altitude.

Health had added a briefing on capacity, with the capacity figures placed in a separate capacity briefing.

Education had added a note saying outcomes were improving in the basket-ready sense.

The Home Office had added another sealed envelope marked CONTEXT FOR CONTEXT.

Treasury had removed the basket lining and booked the saving as delivery.

The basket handle began to strain.

Facilities said this was a physical problem with strategic implications.

Communications proposed calling it a strong basket.

Legal warned that strong could be tested.

Policy suggested dependable.

Treasury asked whether dependable required maintenance.

Digital said the online basket was receiving high engagement because users kept trying to click the thing underneath the blur.

The junior adviser asked whether the government had confused collecting briefings with understanding them.

The Cabinet Secretary said understanding was a later phase, dependent on collection achieving maturity.

## CLIMAX

The press conference began with the Briefing Basket placed beside the lectern.

"This government is focused, organised, and ready to act," the Prime Minister said.

The basket label displayed: ACTION MATERIAL HELD SECURELY.

A reporter asked what action would be taken.

Communications signalled confidence.

Policy signalled caution.

Treasury signalled affordability.

Legal signalled that action was a term with multiple meanings.

The Prime Minister said the basket showed the public that information was being handled responsibly.

The basket displayed: RESPONSIBLE HANDLING UNDER REVIEW.

Another reporter asked whether the basket was simply where difficult briefings went until they became old briefings.

The Cabinet Secretary said that was an unhelpful simplification of a developing prioritisation environment.

Digital announced that the next release would let citizens sort briefings by theme, mood, distance from decision, and likelihood of becoming a ministerial sentence.

The junior adviser said the basket had achieved something important: it had gathered every reason for delay into one portable object.

The Prime Minister concluded that the National Briefing Basket would bring clarity, discipline, and a renewed national confidence that somewhere, in a managed container, the facts were waiting their turn.

## TAG

By evening, departments had requested individual baskets.

Treasury approved one shared handle, provided departments supplied their own hope.

Communications asked whether tomorrow's announcement could promise government with everything in hand.

Legal said only if nobody asked what the hand was holding.

**END**
