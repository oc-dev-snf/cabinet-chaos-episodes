# THE THICK OF ITCH

**Episode:** 206
**Title:** The National Resilience Teaspoon
**Date:** 2026-09-14
**Genre:** Original political satire

---

## COLD OPEN

The Minister enters the Cabinet room to find a single teaspoon on the table.

It is resting on a velvet pad.

A small placard reads **NATIONAL RESILIENCE ASSET**.

"Why is the cutlery being honoured?"

The Permanent Secretary folds his hands.

"It is the National Resilience Teaspoon."

"It is a spoon."

"Yes, Minister. A small, agile instrument for stirring confidence at pace."

The comms director adjusts the lighting around it.

"The visual says practical preparedness."

"The visual says someone has stolen the biscuits and left a clue."

The policy lead checks the briefing.

"The teaspoon demonstrates that government can still move things in difficult conditions."

"What things?"

"Sugar. Potentially powdered milk."

The junior official leans in.

"It has an asset number."

The Minister reads the label.

"NR-TSP-001. Are there others?"

The Permanent Secretary smiles.

"That depends on the spending review."

## ACT ONE

The teaspoon began as a prop for a resilience week photo opportunity.

By breakfast it had become a cross-departmental symbol of national readiness.

By nine thirty it had acquired a delivery board, a stakeholder pack, and a draft standard for stir direction.

The Minister reads from the submission.

"The teaspoon is scalable, familiar, and capable of operating without software."

"So is a doorstop."

"Doorstops are with Infrastructure."

The comms director wants the Minister photographed stirring a mug labelled **BRITAIN PREPARES**.

Legal advises against implying the mug is policy.

Finance asks whether the teaspoon can be shared with existing catering resources.

Security asks whether the teaspoon could be repurposed in a hostile beverage environment.

The Minister puts the paper down.

"We are not launching a spoon."

"Correct," says the Permanent Secretary. "We are launching the capability represented by the spoon."

"Which is?"

"Being able to launch something small."

The junior official raises a hand.

"Catering says the spoon is missing from room 4B."

Everyone looks at the velvet pad.

The Permanent Secretary writes **interdepartmental asset mobility** in his notebook.

## ACT TWO

Departments begin submitting requests to align with the teaspoon.

Health proposes a washable variant.

Transport asks if it can be attached to a timetable.

Education suggests resilience spoons for schools, with age-appropriate stirring outcomes.

The Treasury asks whether departments can demonstrate resilience by not having a teaspoon.

The policy lead unveils the Stirring Readiness Framework.

Level one is awareness of the spoon.

Level two is access to the spoon.

Level three is controlled stirring under supervision.

Level four is independent stirring with lessons learned.

The Minister scans the chart.

"Where is the level for making tea?"

"Tea is an adjacent dependency."

"You have built a framework around a beverage you have not procured."

The comms director says tea is too culturally loaded and suggests warm liquid.

Legal says warm liquid may imply liability.

Finance says liquid is a recurrent cost.

The junior official reports that room 4B has filed a recovery request.

"For the spoon?"

"For morale."

The Permanent Secretary asks whether morale has an asset tag.

No one is certain.

## ACT THREE

By midday the teaspoon is placed inside a clear acrylic case.

The case is locked.

The key is placed in a second case.

The second case is awaiting a procurement note.

The Minister stares at the display.

"Can anyone use the resilience teaspoon?"

"Use is a later phase," says the Permanent Secretary.

"Then what is resilient about it?"

"It has survived scrutiny."

"It is behind plastic."

"Exactly. Protected."

The comms director drafts a line about safeguarding national stirring capacity.

The policy lead says the phrase should be "maintaining optionality over agitation."

The junior official arrives with a message from Catering.

"They say without that spoon, room 4B is down to forks."

The Minister points at the case.

"Then give it back."

The room freezes.

The Permanent Secretary lowers his voice.

"Minister, once an everyday object has been designated strategic, returning it to ordinary use may create a precedent."

"For what?"

"Things helping."

## CLIMAX

The launch takes place in the atrium beside a banner reading **READY FOR ANYTHING PRACTICAL**.

The acrylic case sits on a plinth.

Officials stand around it with the careful expressions of people protecting an idea from its own contents.

A reporter asks whether the country is more resilient than it was yesterday.

The Permanent Secretary offers the prepared line.

"The National Resilience Teaspoon gives the public a clear, relatable symbol of preparedness, coordination, and calm capability."

The Minister looks at the locked case, the missing key, the anxious officials, and the catering supervisor waiting with a mug cooling in his hand.

Then he opens the case with a paperclip.

No one breathes.

He picks up the teaspoon and hands it to the supervisor.

"There. National capability restored."

The comms director whispers that the photo is confusing.

"Good," says the Minister. "It contains an event."

## TAG

Back in the Cabinet room, the velvet pad is empty.

The Permanent Secretary says they will need a declassification minute before the absence can be acknowledged.

The junior official enters with a tray of tea.

Every mug has been stirred.

The Minister takes one.

"Any issues?"

"Only one," says the junior official. "Room 4B wants to know if the spoon can come back as a consultant."

The Minister looks at the empty pad.

"Tell them it already has more practical experience than the framework."

**END**
