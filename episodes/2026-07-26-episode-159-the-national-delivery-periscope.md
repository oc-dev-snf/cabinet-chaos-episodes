# THE THICK OF ITCH

**Episode:** 159
**Title:** The National Delivery Periscope
**Genre:** Original political satire

---

## COLD OPEN

At 07:00, the Prime Minister announced the National Delivery Periscope.

It was described as a practical instrument for seeing over immediate difficulty while remaining fully below the surface of operational detail.

Communications said the public wanted visibility.

Policy said visibility required line of sight.

Legal said line of sight created evidence.

Treasury said evidence created cost pressure.

The Cabinet Secretary explained that the periscope would allow ministers to observe delivery without becoming entangled in the delivery environment.

The junior adviser asked whether looking at a problem meant knowing about it.

Legal said knowledge depended on the angle.

Communications said angles were best described as perspectives.

Digital offered to build a Periscope Dashboard showing emerging issues, partially emerging issues, and issues that had briefly surfaced before returning to a more manageable depth.

The Prime Minister said the country deserved a government prepared to rise above the noise, take a clear look around, and then proceed with disciplined sub-surface momentum.

Everyone agreed this sounded decisive, provided nobody asked why the government was underwater.

## ACT ONE

By 08:30, departments had been asked to submit matters requiring strategic observation.

Transport nominated three timetable risks and one tunnel that preferred not to be observed.

Health submitted a waiting list graph with a note saying it became calmer if viewed from further away.

Education proposed a curriculum review periscope so schools could see the next announcement before it saw them.

The Home Office submitted a sealed tube marked VISIBILITY SENSITIVE.

Treasury submitted a model proving that looking at problems was cheaper than touching them.

Communications rejected several submissions for using the word delay.

Policy said delay was sometimes the thing being observed.

Communications said that made it sound too close.

The Cabinet Secretary proposed a standard sighting protocol:

ONE ISSUE ON THE HORIZON.

TWO LINES OF RESPONSIBLE DISTANCE.

THREE OPTIONS FOR FURTHER OBSERVATION.

NO SPLASHING.

Legal asked whether a splash counted as ministerial involvement.

Treasury asked whether involvement could be capitalised.

The junior adviser asked what would happen if the periscope showed the same problem every day.

The Cabinet Secretary said that would demonstrate continuity.

Communications said continuity polled better than recurrence.

## ACT TWO

At 10:00, Digital unveiled the first version of the Periscope Dashboard.

VISIBLE CONCERN: PRESENT.

MINISTERIAL DEPTH: APPROPRIATE.

DELIVERY CURRENT: COMPLEX.

SURFACE CONFIDENCE: STABLE PENDING WAVES.

Communications liked waves because they sounded natural.

Policy said some waves were caused by decisions.

Legal said causation should remain submerged until required.

Treasury asked whether the dashboard could show confidence without showing why confidence was needed.

Digital added a dial called HORIZONAL GRIP.

The Cabinet Secretary said horizontal was not a word.

Digital said it tested well with stakeholders who wanted direction but disliked vertical accountability.

The Prime Minister rehearsed the launch line.

"We are looking directly at delivery."

Legal objected to directly.

Communications objected to delivery.

Treasury objected to looking, because looking often preceded spending.

The revised line became: "We are maintaining a clear strategic view of the conditions in which delivery can be responsibly understood."

Everyone agreed it was stronger because it was harder to remember.

## ACT THREE

At midday, the National Delivery Periscope Board met beside an actual brass periscope borrowed from an exhibition.

Communications said it added authenticity.

Policy said it added metaphor.

Legal said it added handling risk.

Treasury asked whether the exhibition expected it back before the spending review.

The Prime Minister asked who would use it first.

The Cabinet Secretary explained that the periscope was not for personal use. It was a collective viewing asset supporting system-wide awareness without individual over-identification.

Digital updated the dashboard:

VIEWING MODE: SHARED.

ACCOUNTABILITY DEPTH: BALANCED.

EMERGENCE RISK: MONITORED.

KNOWN UNKNOWNS: VISIBLE IN PRINCIPLE.

The junior adviser looked through the periscope and said it appeared to be pointed at the Cabinet Room ceiling.

Communications said that was aspirational.

Policy said it showed upward trajectory.

Legal said ceilings were safer than horizons because they contained fewer commitments.

Treasury asked whether upward trajectory required a lift maintenance budget.

The Cabinet Secretary ordered the periscope rotated toward the window, then immediately convened a working group on what might be seen.

## CLIMAX

The afternoon press conference began with the Prime Minister beside a lectern marked DELIVERY IN VIEW.

"Today, we are giving the public a clearer view of progress," the Prime Minister said.

A reporter asked what progress could currently be seen.

Communications signalled "emerging".

Policy signalled "complex".

Legal signalled "do not describe the object".

The Prime Minister said progress was visible as part of a wider field of practical movement.

Another reporter asked whether the periscope had identified any problems.

The Cabinet Secretary stepped forward and explained that a problem, once responsibly observed, became an insight, and an insight became a basis for future grip.

Digital refreshed the dashboard.

It displayed:

PROBLEMS: TRANSITIONING TO INSIGHTS.

INSIGHTS: ENTERING FRAMEWORK.

FRAMEWORK: MAINTAINING DEPTH.

PUBLIC VIEW: CALIBRATED.

The reporter asked whether this meant the government had seen the problem and chosen not to act.

Legal said observation should not be confused with acceptance.

Communications said visibility had never been more active.

Policy said active visibility was the first stage of delivery confidence.

Treasury said confidence should be funded only after visibility produced savings.

The junior adviser said the government had invented a periscope so it could claim to be looking over the top while keeping its head down.

The Prime Minister concluded that the National Delivery Periscope proved the government was focused, forward-looking, and fully committed to seeing what could be seen when conditions allowed.

## TAG

By evening, departments were asked to submit periscope sightings.

Most reported that they had seen progress on the horizon, although several admitted it may have been the reflection of the launch banner.
